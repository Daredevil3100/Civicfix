How Resolution Works

1
Citizen Submission

Report is verified, assigned a tracking ID, and pinned to the public registry.

2
Department Triage

The relevant municipal department (Roads, Water, Waste) receives and logs the ticket.

3
Field Action & Repair

Maintenance crews are dispatched. Status updates to In Progress.

4
Inspection & Closure

Work is inspected, photos verified, and marked Resolved for citizen confirmation.

Emergency Helpline Notice

For live fire hazards, active electrical explosions, severe gas leaks, or medical emergencies, do not wait for online ticketing. Call emergency services immediately (911 / 112).
